mediatek: filogic: keep MT7988 PCIe PEXTP clocks enabled

The PEXTP reference clock selectors pextp_p0_sel..pextp_p3_sel have no
consumer in the MT7988 device tree, so clk_disable_unused() gates them.
On the BananaPi BPI-R4 Pro 8X the deferred, asynchronous mtk-pcie-gen3
probe overlaps with clk_disable_unused(), and links randomly fail with
"PCIe link down" (-110), dropping NVMe SSDs and the Wi-Fi card.

Mark the four clocks critical, as MediaTek's SDK does. Tested on a
BPI-R4 Pro 8X with BE14 and two NVMe SSDs: 2/6 boots with all links up
before, 16/16 (10 warm, 5 cold, 1 after sysupgrade) with this patch.
